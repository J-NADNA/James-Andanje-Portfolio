ABOUT ME

More than calendar management.

My approach to executive assistance is centered around making an executive's working day easier, more organized and more productive.

That means looking beyond individual tasks and understanding the bigger picture: what needs to happen next, what information is required, which stakeholders need a response and what can be handled before it becomes an issue.

I support executives across scheduling, travel, research, communications, meeting preparation, expenses, CRM administration and day-to-day operational coordination.

WHAT I DO

Executive support that keeps things moving.

Coordinating flights, accommodation, ground transportation, itineraries and the details required for smooth business travel.

Preparing executives for meetings through agendas, background research, attendee information, materials and clearly organized follow-up actions.
